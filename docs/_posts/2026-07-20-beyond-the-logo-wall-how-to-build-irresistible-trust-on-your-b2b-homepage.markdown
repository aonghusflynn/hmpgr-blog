Most B2B websites have a row of silver logos halfway down the page. 

You have seen them. The "Trusted by" section. For many founders, this is a "set it and forget it" task. You grab a few client logos, paste them into a slider, and assume the job is done.

Logos prove you have had customers, but they do not prove you can solve a new visitor's specific problem. To turn a skeptic into a lead, move beyond the logo wall. Build evidence that converts.

## Stop using "wall of text" testimonials

Long testimonials are often ignored. Busy prospects scan pages rather than reading every word. If a quote looks like a paragraph, eyes will jump over it.

### Highlight the result
Instead of a generic "They were great to work with," use quotes that mention a specific result. 

### Use the "power headline" method
Do not just list a name and a quote. Add a bold headline above the testimonial that summarizes the win. 

*Example:* **"Reduced our churn by 22% in the first quarter."** followed by the client quote.

## Match your proof to your claims

Build trust by matching evidence to your promises. If a headline claims the software is "Easy to use," but testimonials only mention "Great customer support," the message fails.

### Place evidence near friction points
Place social proof next to the spots where people hesitate rather than only in a dedicated section.

*   **Near your CTA button:** Add a micro-testimonial or a "Join 500+ other founders" note.
*   **Next to your pricing:** Show a badge or a quote about the ROI.
*   **On your contact form:** List your average response time or a rating from review sites.

## Use data as proof

Logos are subjective. Data is objective. B2B buyers need to justify purchases to their bosses. Give them the numbers to do it.

Instead of saying "We help you grow," try "Our average client sees a 30% increase in lead volume within 60 days."

### The micro-stat technique
You do not need a massive case study to use data. Use small stats throughout the page:
*   "98% customer satisfaction rate."
*   "Onboarding takes less than 15 minutes."
*   "Over 10,000 reports generated this month."

### Pro tip: the human factor
Use a real headshot next to a quote. Research shows that humans look at faces. A testimonial with a clear face and a verified job title carries more weight than an anonymous quote from "John D."

## Audit your evidence regularly

Trust decays. A testimonial from 2021 suggests your best days are behind you. 

Refresh your social proof every six months. Ensure the companies you feature are relevant to the target audience you want to attract today.
