Most B2B websites suffer from the "Curse of Knowledge."

You know your product inside out. You know the technical specs and the hours of coding. Naturally, you write about these things on your website.

Your customer does not care how the clock is made. They just want to know what time it is. To increase conversions, shift from selling features to selling outcomes.

## The "so what?" test
Every time you describe a feature on your website, ask yourself: "So what?"

If your website says, "We have a real-time data dashboard," the customer asks, "So what?" 
The answer should be: "So you can stop wasting three hours a week on manual reporting."

### Audit your current copy
Look at your homepage. Count how many times you use the word "We" or "Our" versus "You" or "Your." 

If "We" wins, your website is a monologue. To convert, it must be a conversation about your customer's success.

## Flip the script
Turn features into benefits by shifting the perspective of the entire page.

### 1. The hero section
Instead of saying "The #1 Project Management Software for Architects," try "Build more, manage less. Get your blueprints approved 30% faster."

### 2. The feature list
Explain that your customer can "Sync all your existing tools in five minutes without writing a single line of code" rather than just listing "API Integration."

### 3. The call to action
A button that says "Submit" or "Schedule Demo" is a chore. A button that says "Start Saving Time" or "Get My Free Report" is a reward.

## Speak the language of the result
B2B buyers are often under pressure. They have bosses to answer to and budgets to justify. They are not looking for a "platform"; they want a promotion or a way to hit their quarterly targets.

Addressing specific results makes the price less relevant. You are a solution rather than a line item.

### Pro tip: the "before and after" visual
Show the contrast between current chaos and future clarity with a simple graphic rather than standard software screenshots. People process visuals faster than text. If they can see the outcome, they are closer to signing up.
