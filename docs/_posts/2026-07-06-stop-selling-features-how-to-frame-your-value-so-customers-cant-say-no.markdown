Most B2B websites read like a product manual. 

They list technical specifications and brag about their architecture. Customers buy solutions to their problems, not features. 

Focusing on what your product does for the user prevents you from leaving revenue on the table. Shift your messaging to drive more conversions.

## The feature trap

Founders are proud of what they built. It is natural to want to show off the hard work. 

A visitor landing on your site has one question: 'How does this make my life easier?'

If they have to work too hard to find that answer, they leave. Features tell people what the tool includes. Outcomes tell people why it matters.

### Feature vs. outcome: a quick comparison
*   **Feature:** "We have a 24/7 automated reporting dashboard."
*   **Outcome:** "Never spend another Sunday night manually building spreadsheets."

The outcome speaks to a specific pain point: losing personal time to tedious tasks. That is what sells.

## The 'so what?' framework

To optimize your website for conversion, you need to audit your current copy. Look at every heading and bullet point on your homepage. 

Ask yourself: "So what?"

If your headline says, "Integrates with over 500 apps," ask yourself why that matters. The answer is likely: "So you can keep your existing workflow without any downtime." That is your new headline.

### Step 1: identify the pain
Before you talk about yourself, acknowledge the visitor's struggle. Are they losing leads or is their team inefficient? 

### Step 2: show the 'after' picture
Describe the world after they use your product. Use active verbs. Replace "Efficiency is improved" with "Get your team's project time back."

### Step 3: use concrete numbers
Generalities are the enemy of conversion. Say you "reduce onboarding from weeks to hours" rather than just "saving time."

## Pro tip: the 'customer-first' swap
Scan your website for the words "We," "Us," and "Our." Try to replace as many as possible with "You" and "Your." 

**Before:** "We provide the best analytics in the SaaS industry."
**After:** "You get the data you need to make confident growth decisions."

This small shift changes the focus from your ego to your customer's success.

## How to audit your message today

You don't need a full website redesign to see results. You can start by updating your hero section (the top part of your homepage). 

1.  **Change your H1:** Focus on the single biggest result you provide.
2.  **Clarify your sub-headline:** Explain exactly who you help and how.
3.  **Refine your CTA:** Make it specific to the value. Instead of "Submit," try "Start saving time."
