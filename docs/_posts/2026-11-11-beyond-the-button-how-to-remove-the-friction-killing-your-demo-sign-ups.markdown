You've spent thousands on ads. You've polished your hero section. You've followed the "5-second rule." But your demo requests are still flat. 

The problem is usually the friction you've built into your conversion path. Friction is anything that makes a user pause or doubt. In B2B SaaS, friction is a silent lead killer. 

Find those hurdles and remove them.

## The cost of asking for too much

Most B2B founders want every possible data point. They want the company size, the job title, the annual revenue, and the phone number. Every field you add to a form drops your conversion rate. 

### Prune your forms
Look at your "Book a Demo" form right now. Ask yourself: "Do I actually need this info to start a conversation?" 

If the answer is no, delete the field. You can gather more data once the lead is in your CRM. For now, the goal is the connection.

## Tell them exactly what happens next

People hate uncertainty. When a visitor clicks your call-to-action (CTA), they are taking a risk. They worry about being hounded by a pushy salesperson or entering a never-ending email sequence.

### Remove the mystery
Add a tiny line of text below your button to explain the process.
- We will send a calendar link instantly.
- A 15-minute intro call with no pressure.

When people know the next step, they are much more likely to take it.

## Show, don't just tell

B2B buyers are tired of generic "all-in-one" platforms. These are empty words. Friction happens when a user cannot visualize how your tool works. If they have to guess what your dashboard looks like, they will probably leave.

### Use action-oriented visuals
Stop using stock photos of people in suits shaking hands. Show the product with a high-quality screenshot or a 10-second looping video. Let the user see the value before they even talk to your team.

## Pro tip: the "one-click" advantage
If you are targeting existing users or newsletter subscribers, don't make them fill out a form they've filled out before. Use "magic links" or pre-filled form fields. Reducing the work to a single click can increase your conversion rates by over 30%.

## Audit your own path

Optimization is a habit. Walk through your own website as if you were a skeptical prospect. Where do you get confused? That is where your growth is leaking.
