Most B2B websites make a fatal mistake. They treat every visitor like they are ready to buy right now.

You’ve seen it everywhere. The primary button is always "Book a Demo" or "Talk to Sales." While these are important, they are "high-friction" asks. 

In reality, 95% of your website visitors are just researching. If your only call to action (CTA) is a 30-minute sales call, they will simply leave. 

To grow your pipeline, you need the "Soft Ask."

## Why High-Friction CTAs Fail

A sales demo is a big commitment. It requires a calendar invite, a Zoom link, and 30 minutes of a busy manager’s time. 

If a visitor doesn't fully understand your value yet, they won't commit. By only offering a "hard sell" option, you lose the chance to build a relationship with potential customers who are still in the "problem-aware" stage.

## Introducing Low-Friction CTAs

A low-friction CTA gives the visitor value without requiring a major time commitment. It allows them to "test drive" your expertise or your product’s logic.

### 1. The Interactive Calculator
Instead of telling people they will save money, let them calculate it. A "ROI Calculator" or "Time-Saved Estimator" is highly engaging. It provides immediate, personalized value.

### 2. The Checklists and Templates
B2B buyers love tools that make them look good to their boss. Offer a "Budget Approval Template" or a "Quarterly Strategy Checklist." These are easy to download and highly relevant to their daily work.

### 3. The "No-Email" Tool
Try offering a small piece of your product’s value for free without a signup. For hmpgr, this might be a quick headline grader. When users see the value first, they are much more likely to hand over their email address later.

## How to Balance Your CTAs

You don't have to remove your "Book a Demo" button. You just need to give it a neighbor.

### Use the "Primary vs. Secondary" Framework
Keep your "Book a Demo" button in the top right of your header. This is for the 5% who are ready to buy.

For everyone else, place a "Soft Ask" button directly below your main hero headline. 

**Example:**
*   **Primary (Bold Color):** Get Started Now
*   **Secondary (Outline Style):** See the 2-Minute Interactive Walkthrough

## Pro Tip: The "Thank You" Page Pivot
The best time to ask for a "high-friction" action is immediately after a "low-friction" success. 

If someone downloads your "B2B Marketing Checklist," don't just show a generic "Check your email" message. On the Thank You page, say: *"While you wait for your checklist, would you like a 5-minute video teardown of your current strategy?"* 

The visitor is already in "action mode." This is your best chance to move them down the funnel.
