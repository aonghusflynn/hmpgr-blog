Most B2B websites make the same mistake. They treat first-time visitors like lifelong partners. They request a demo before the visitor knows what the product does.

In the B2B world, the "Book a Demo" button often kills conversions. It signals a 30-minute commitment, a sales pitch, and a long follow-up sequence. For a busy manager, that feels like a lot of work.

To increase conversions, lower the barrier to entry.

## The commitment gap

Visitors land on your site looking for answers. If your only call to action (CTA) is a demo request, you ignore the 95% of visitors not yet ready for a sales call. You leave money on the table by not offering engagement on their terms.

To fix this, use low-friction alternatives.

## Three low-friction alternatives to the demo

### 1. The 2-minute product tour
Let visitors see the product immediately instead of asking them to talk to a human. A recorded video tour allows the visitor to self-qualify. If they like what they see, they are more likely to book a demo later.

### 2. The interactive sandbox
B2B buyers like to click things. An interactive sandbox or a clickable prototype lets the user experience your tool without signing up. This builds trust.

### 3. The "no-email" case study
Stop gating every piece of content. If you have a success story, let people read it. If the results are strong, they will find the contact button.

## How to layer your CTAs

You don't have to delete your "Book a Demo" button. Just give it a neighbor. Use a primary and secondary CTA layout. 

*   **Primary CTA:** Book a Demo (High intent, high friction).
*   **Secondary CTA:** Watch the Tour (Low intent, low friction).

This captures leads at different stages of the buying journey. 

## Pro tip: the "what happens next" text
People avoid "Book a Demo" buttons because they fear the unknown. Add a line of micro-copy below your button. 

Example: "30-minute walkthrough. No credit card required. We'll show you how to save 10 hours a week." 

Defining the outcome and time commitment removes the scare factor and increases clicks.
