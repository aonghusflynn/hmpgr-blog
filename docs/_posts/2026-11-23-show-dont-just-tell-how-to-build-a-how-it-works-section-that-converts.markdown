B2B buyers are tired of guessing. 

They visit your site, see your headline about AI, and look at your logo wall. But they still have one question: 

"What actually happens after I sign up?"

If your website does not answer this, you lose leads to competitors who do. Clarity closes deals. 

## The black box problem

Most B2B websites suffer from a black box problem. They describe results but hide the process. 

When a visitor cannot see how to use your software, they feel risk. Risk kills conversion. A "how it works" section connects their problem to your solution.

## Three steps to a high-converting process section

Use a simple three-step roadmap instead of a long manual.

### 1. The integration (the start)
Show the first step. Whether it is a five-minute setup or a data import, make the beginning feel easy. 

### 2. The heavy lifting (the middle)
Explain what your product does while they sleep. This demonstrates value. Focus on the automation or expertise that saves them time.

### 3. The big win (the result)
End with the outcome. Tell them they will receive a weekly report that shows exactly where their team wastes money.

## Speak to your customer's workflow, not your features

Talk about their Tuesday morning instead of your database speed. 

B2B buyers care about how your tool fits into their existing stack. Use active verbs: Connect, Sync, Automate, Review. 

Describing their struggle and how you remove it in three steps wins half the battle.

### Pro tip: The screenshot shortcut
Use screenshots of your product UI instead of generic icons. Crop images to show only the most relevant dashboard elements. This makes the product feel real before they book a demo.
